5 Minutes to the Beach, Steps to Everything

September 2026 • Author: NSB Hideaway

The Hideaway sits in historic downtown New Smyrna Beach. From the property you can walk to shops, restaurants, and the weekly Saturday Farmers Market held at Old Fort Park on Sams Avenue (typically 7:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.). RiverWalk Park and the Intracoastal are nearby. The Atlantic is a consistent five-minute drive.

This combination of walkable downtown character and quick beach access is one of the strongest reasons guests choose the property over oceanfront condos. You keep the privacy of a private pool while still being able to stroll to dinner or the market.
