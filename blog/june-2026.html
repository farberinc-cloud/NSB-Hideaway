What to Do in Historic Downtown NSB

June 2026 • Author: NSB Hideaway

From The Hideaway you can walk to the New Smyrna Beach Farmers Market, held every Saturday at Old Fort Park (around 210 Sams Avenue) from approximately 7:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. The market has operated since 1991 and features local produce, baked goods, plants, and artisans.

Flagler Avenue, RiverWalk Park, and the Intracoastal Waterway are also nearby. Monthly classic car shows and other downtown events add to the walkable character of the area. The beach remains a short five-minute drive.
