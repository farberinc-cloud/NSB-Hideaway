An Easy Drive from the Greater Orlando Area

From Casselberry or Sanford, New Smyrna Beach is a direct drive, not a trip that needs a flight plan. Most guests reach The Hideaway in about an hour, sometimes a little less from Sanford and closer to the 45- to 75-minute range if you are coming through the wider Orlando suburbs. The reward is a private saltwater pool bungalow in historic downtown, not a shared beach condo.

People searching for a weekend trip from Casselberry to the beach, or a Sanford to New Smyrna Beach rental, want two answers: how long the car ride is, and whether the house is worth unpacking for two nights. The ride is one stretch east. The house is meticulously clean, with your own pool, soaking tub, rain shower, and fire table. Guests are 30 or older. The beach is about five minutes after you arrive. Downtown, including the Saturday Farmers Market at Old Fort Park, is walkable.

Orlando Sanford International is the practical airport if you are flying into the north side of the region and driving over. Daytona Beach International is the closer field if you are coming from the coast. Either way you end at the same downtown bungalow. It was remodeled in 2019 and operates only as a vacation rental, so arrival is a code and a private yard, not a front desk.

A few house rules keep that clean, easy feeling in place: no pets, no smoking, no parties, and the guest who books stays. Seven-night reservations include a $200 gift card to The Garlic at 556 E 3rd Avenue, about five minutes from the property and open daily from 4:00 p.m. A two-night stay still has the pool. The card is there when you can stretch the calendar.

If you are leaving Casselberry or Sanford after work, book the Friday arrival on Airbnb first. The pool will be private when you get there, which is the reason to drive an hour. Check the dates, confirm you meet the 30-and-older rule, and reserve before that weekend is gone.
