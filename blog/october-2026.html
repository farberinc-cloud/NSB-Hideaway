Private Pool Oasis in Historic Downtown NSB

October 2026 • Author: NSB Hideaway

The Hideaway offers something rare in New Smyrna Beach: a fully private saltwater pool, outdoor soaking tub, rain shower, and gas fire table, all within a dedicated vacation bungalow in the historic downtown area.

Guests consistently rate the property 5.0 stars. The house was completely remodeled in 2019 and continues to deliver the same carefully maintained experience. Because the outdoor spaces are private, you can enjoy the pool and fire table without sharing amenities with other guests.

Location adds to the appeal. The property sits near Live Oak Street, steps from shops, restaurants, the Saturday Farmers Market at Old Fort Park (210 Sams Avenue), and RiverWalk Park. The beach is a short five-minute drive away.

House rules keep the oasis peaceful: no smoking, no pets, and no parties. Guests must be 30 or older, and the person who books must stay on the property.
